DAY 1
Time
Core Amritsar + Wagah
9:00 AM- 9:15 AM
Arrive in the city, check into your accommodation. Prefer hotel near Golden Temple or Railway Station.
9:15 AM-10:00 AM
Have breakfast at nearby option Bharawan Da Dhaba or somewhere else. Eat famous Chole Puri and Lassi.
10:00 AM to 12:00 PM
Visit the most famous Golden Temple. Take a dip in Amrit Sarovar and experience Langar (free community meal).
12:00 PM to 1:00 PM
Visit nearby Jallianwala Bagh which is at the walking distance from golden temple. It is the historical site of the tragic massacre.
1:15 PM to 2:30 PM
Visit Gobindgarh Fort which is good for Cultural shows, Punjabi vibes and light snacks after jallianwala bagh.
3:00 PM to 7:00 PM
Visit the Wagah Border after taking rest and try to leave early then only you get best seats and get to experience the high energy.
7:30 PM to 8:30 PM
Visit the famous Durgiana Temple after coming back from wagah border and get to experience the warmth of sprituality.
9:00 PM to 10:00 PM
Eat dinner nearby and must try the famous Dal Makhani and Paneer Dishes.
10:00 PM to 11:30 PM
Go for a walk at Heritage street and get to experience the night golden view of amritsar. Visit Partition Museum there and view Maharaja Ranjit Singh Statue in the middle of the road. Try Amritsar famous Jalebi and Faluda Ice Cream.
DAY 2
Time
Spiritual + Cultural + Food + Local Exploration
6:00 AM to 8:30 AM
Revisit Golden Temple if you can early morning is one of the best experience and then visit Mata Kaulan Sahib and Baba Atal Sahib gurudwara nearby. Have your morning tea at Giani Tea Stall in between.
9:00 AM to 11:00 AM
Must try famous Amritsari Kulcha nearby somewhere for breakfast. After breakfast come back to hotel and checkout.
11:00 AM to 12:30 PM
Visit Mata Lal Devi Temple where you get to see various symbolic structures representing different spiritual experiences.
12:30 PM to 2:30 PM
Visit Sadda Pind where you get to experience traditional punjabi culture. Visit Chheharta Sahib Gurudwara which will be on your way.
3:00 PM to 5:00 PM
Visit Ram Tirath which is one of the important religious and cultural site. You can also visit Pipli Sahib Gurudwara on route.
5:30 PM to 7:30 PM
Visit the famous and historic Kesar Da Dhaba then visit the hidden places nearby Guru Ka Mahal and Bhai Shalo Da Tobha which is at the walking distance from there.
7:30 PM to 10:00 PM
Visit local markets like Hall Bazar, Guru Bazar, Katra Jaimal Singh Market for souveniers and must buy Punjabi Jutti, Phulkari Suit and Papad Wadiyan.
10:00 PM to 11:00 PM
Have your dinner at famous restaurant in the middle of the bazar RK Food Plaza and can leave on time back to your original location.

* Points to Remember
  • Don’t miss Amritsar’s street food — especially near Golden Temple and Hall Bazaar.
  • Must-try: Amritsari Kulcha, Chole Puri, Pede wali Lassi, Jalebi, Faluda, Aam Papad.
  • Visit Golden Temple early morning (5–7 AM) or late night for best experience.
  • Reach Wagah Border at least 1–1.5 hours early.
  • Most attractions near Golden Temple are walking distance.
  • Bargaining is common in local markets.
  • Must Buy: Phulkari, Punjabi Jutti, Papad Wadiyan, Spritual Items.
  • Respect religious places & maintain decorum.
