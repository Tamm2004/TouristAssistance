About Amrit Path

Home / Contact Us

Amrit Path is a dedicated tourist assistance platform designed to help visitors explore the rich culture, heritage, food, and traditions of Amritsar — popularly known as Guru Ki Nagri. Our goal is to provide tourists with accurate, reliable, and well-organized information about famous places, historical monuments, traditional markets, restaurants, hotels, and local products — all in one place. We aim to make every visitor’s journey smooth, informed, and memorable.

Our Mission

To create a centralized digital guide for tourists visiting Amritsar.

To support local businesses by giving them a platform to reach more customers.

To reduce dependency on expensive tourist guides through digital assistance.

To promote local culture, heritage, and traditions.

Our Vision

Our vision is to expand this platform beyond Amritsar and develop it into a comprehensive, multi-city tourist assistance system across India. We aspire to create a smart and reliable digital companion that offers personalized recommendations, real-time assistance, and authentic local insights. By combining technology with cultural awareness, we aim to promote sustainable tourism, support small businesses, and enhance the overall travel experience for future generations.

How We Help!

Detailed information about famous places and monuments.

Authentic food recommendations with locations.

Trusted hotel and stay options.

Traditional shopping guidance and souvenir suggestions.

Ratings and reviews for better decision-making.

AI-based chatbot and smart recommendations.
